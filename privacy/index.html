Privacy Policy

The Tab — Chrome extension · Effective date: May 12, 2026

This policy describes how The Tab (“the extension”) handles information when you install and use it. The extension replaces Chrome’s default new tab page with a personalized dashboard and optional tools described below.

Who we are

The Tab is published by the developer identified on the item’s Chrome Web Store page. For privacy questions, use the contact email or support link on that listing (the Store requires a way for users to reach you).

What the extension processes

The extension may process the following categories of data on your device or through services you trigger:

  • Settings and local state — Preferences (theme, layout options, blocking lists, feature toggles, cached weather responses, optional encrypted keys for local summaries) stored with Chrome’s chrome.storage APIs.
  • Browsing history (read-only via Chrome) — With your permission, the extension uses Chrome’s History API to show suggestions, insights, and shortcuts derived from pages you visited. History data is processed inside the extension and is not uploaded to our servers (we do not operate a backend for this product unless separately disclosed).
  • Tab activity / dwell time — Optional summaries of time spent on sites may be derived from active tab URLs and titles and stored locally (including encrypted storage where implemented) for dashboard analytics.
  • Location-related signals (for weather) — If you enable weather or location-based modes, the extension may use coarse network-based location, browser geolocation when you allow it, or geocoding/reverse geocoding services to show a label and forecast. Those requests go directly from your browser to third-party endpoints required for that feature.
  • Optional Google account — If you choose to connect Google, Chrome’s Identity API may obtain an OAuth token so the extension can request basic profile fields (such as name, email, and profile picture) from Google’s userinfo endpoint. Profile data may be cached locally for a short period to avoid repeated requests.
  • Search suggestions — When you type in the search bar, the extension may request query suggestions from Google’s suggestion service over HTTPS.

Third-party services

Network requests initiated by the extension may reach services needed for optional features, including but not limited to: Google (OAuth userinfo, token revocation, query suggestions), Open‑Meteo and related geocoding endpoints for weather, and IP or reverse‑geocode helpers used only to resolve approximate location or city labels for weather. Those providers process requests under their own policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for creditworthiness or lending.
  • We do not run unrelated ads or profiling beyond operating the extension’s stated features.

Data retention

Data stored locally follows your browser and extension storage until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Cached OAuth profile data uses a short time-to-live where implemented. You can revoke Google access through your Google account settings and remove cached tokens via the extension’s sign‑out flows where provided.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension changes. The effective date at the top will be revised. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

This page is hosted for Chrome Web Store disclosure. Extension behavior always follows the version you install from the Store.
